# Vindecode design direction

Vindecode helps vehicle buyers understand report options before making a purchase. The landing page should feel calm, thoughtful, and straightforward, taking cues from Claude's warm, human-centered interface without copying its identity.

Use a warm paper background, charcoal text, restrained terracotta actions, a literary serif for prominent headings, and a clean sans serif for controls and body text. Photography shows the vehicles; data and forms should be legible and practical. Favor open spacing and quiet separators over decorative panels. Avoid ornamental motion and unsupported claims.

Maintain visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, comfortable touch targets, readable contrast, narrow-screen layouts and reduced-motion support. Preserve the existing report information and interactive behavior.
